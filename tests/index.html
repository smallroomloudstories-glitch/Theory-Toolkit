Theory Toolkit — Current Builds

These are the latest builds that have completed functional testing. They are not the finished Theory Toolkit interface.

Thanks for helping test Theory Toolkit!

Theory Toolkit is being developed as a visual companion for music students and instructors. The goal is to make musical relationships easier to see and explore while an instructor explains them — and to give students something they can return to later when practicing or reviewing.

Right now, these pages are functional prototypes, not UI or visual-design previews. Please don't worry much about whether they are pretty. I'm testing whether the controls, behavior, and musical information make sense before building the finished interface.

Please explore rather than following a strict test script. Change selections, try combinations, and see whether the page behaves the way you expect it to.

The feedback I'm most interested in is:

  • What worked? What felt clear, useful, or intuitive?
  • What didn't? Did anything behave incorrectly, confusingly, or awkwardly?
  • What did you expect? If something surprised you, what did you think would happen instead?
  • What would you like to see added? Is there a control, comparison, view, or piece of information that would make this more useful to you?

You do not need to be a music-theory expert to give useful feedback. If something doesn't make sense to you, that itself is useful information. If you are an instructor or experienced player, I'm also very interested in anything that is musically misleading or that you would present differently to a student.
